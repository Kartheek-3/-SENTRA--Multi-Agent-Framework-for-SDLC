# WHO Digital Health Interventions Guidance

Engineering use:
- Digital health systems should support, not replace, health-system workflows.
- Consider feasibility, equity, safety, and implementation context when designing health software.
- Treat health data and patient workflows as sensitive and safety-relevant.

SEAM application:
- Healthcare planning should include actor roles, privacy, auditability, and workflow constraints.
- QA should verify that patient-facing actions and staff actions are separated by authorization.
- Delivery documentation should preserve known limitations and assumptions.
